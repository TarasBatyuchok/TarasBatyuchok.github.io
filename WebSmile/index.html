Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about working with WebSmile.

Frequently Asked Questions

01What services does WebSmile provide?

We design and develop landing pages, corporate websites, and online stores. We also help plan a clear site structure and build a solid technical SEO foundation.

02Can you help a small or local business?

Yes. We create websites for small and medium-sized businesses, including local service providers, with each project shaped around its goals and audience.

03What types of websites do you build?

Projects range from focused landing pages to company websites and online stores. We can discuss the right format once we understand what your visitors need to do.

04How long does a website project take?

The timeline depends on the scope, content, and feedback cycle. We agree on stages and a schedule before work begins.

05Do you provide support after launch?

We can discuss updates and ongoing support as part of your project plan so the website stays useful as your business changes.

06Can you work with an existing design?

We can review your existing design, content, or website and suggest how to build on what is already working.

07How involved will I be in the process?

We involve you at key decisions, share progress, and make room for feedback throughout design and development.

08Will my website have an SEO foundation?

We build with clear page structure, responsive layouts, and technical basics that help search engines understand your site. Search rankings depend on many factors beyond development.
